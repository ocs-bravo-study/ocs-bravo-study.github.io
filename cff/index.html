        Cheat sheet · Call for fire grid mission

        The worksheet on screen is the paper "Call For Fire Grid Mission" sheet. Each line is one transmission with its own Send. Struck labels are printed struck on the sheet: write the value, do not say the label.

        Phase 1 · Orient

        1. Start position: the evaluator gives a 6-digit grid. Find the square (read right, then up), then the tenths inside it, and mark ◎ on the map. Grid line numbers are the yellow circles; a big green number is a training area, not a grid number.
        2. Direction: the 3D view has a mil scale across its top (0–6400, magnetic). Read the number straight above the target (TGT n in the yellow circle) and add the G-M angle 70 mils = grid direction, to the nearest 10 mils → line 2 Direction (6370 + 70 = 6440 → 0040). 1° = 17.78 mils, because 360° = 6400 mils.
        3. Mark ✕: the 3D view shows a few red numbers, each next to a landmark you can find on the map (a farm, where the dirt road leaves the main road, a railroad crossing, the railroad cut, the big hill); the target is one of them. The map draws your Direction from ◎ as your protractor line: find the target's landmark along it, press ✕ Target under the map and tap next to it.
        4. Distance: the protractor's range scale ◎→✕ lies from ◎ through ✕, a tick every 100 m: read where ✕ sits, nearest 100 m → DISTANCE.
        5. OT factor: distance in thousands, nearest whole number: 1200 → 1.2 → 1.
        6. Read the Grid at ✕ with the protractor, 8 digits (4 right, then 4 up) → line 2. ALT is printed: 160.

        Phase 2 · Call: line 1, then lines 2 and 3 as one call, each ends OVER

        HAMMER 3, THIS IS STEEL 2, ADJUST FIRE, OVER
        GRID GA 6521 2143, ALT 160, DIRECTION 5620, 1 TANK STATIONARY IN THE OPEN, REQUEST SPLASH, OVER

        Send line 1, then lines 2 and 3 together: the FDC does not read them back. Rule: every FDC message that ends OVER needs an answer; OUT needs none.

        Phase 3 · Line 4: message to observer

        The FDC tells you the MTO (MESSAGE TO OBSERVER, ANVIL, 2 ROUNDS, TARGET NUMBER AH1581, OVER). Write Unit to Fire, # of Rounds and TGT # on line 4 (Changes to CFF is printed N/A), then read it back to the FDC ending OUT. Then SHOT, OVER → SHOT, OUT; SPLASH, OVER → SPLASH, OUT (SPLASH = 5 s to impact, 2 s on Fast: eyes on the target).

        Phase 4 · Adjust: one row per round

        • Deviation correction: count the mils from the vertical line to the burst × OT factor, nearest 10 m, written XX L or XX R (the side opposite the burst). 25 m or less: leave it blank (close enough left/right), so a correction is at least 30 m; once a row is blank, no more L/R corrections for the mission. Near the 25 m line (OT × mils within OT × 5 of 25) blank or 30 both pass: you count the mils yourself.
        • Range correction: burst more than 50 m short (in front) → Add, more than 50 m over (beyond) → Drop. 800 is struck out: the first range correction is 400, then each one is half of the last: 200, 100, 50FFE. 50FFE = fire for effect. In class that is 4 rows: L/R + 400, 200, 100, 50FFE.
        • Each row starts with TARGET and the number from the MTO, printed on the row (nothing to tap): TARGET AH1581, RIGHT 40, DROP 400, OVER.
        • No read-back: the FDC fires at once: SHOT, OVER → SHOT, OUT; SPLASH, OVER → SPLASH, OUT. 5 rows: rows 1–4 can adjust; fire for effect (50FFE) on row 5 at the latest.
        • A burst within 50 m of the target's range (range correct) → leave Add/Drop blank and tap 50FFE.

        Phase 5 · End of mission

        ROUNDS COMPLETE, OVER → ROUNDS COMPLETE, OUT. Then End of Mission: write the TGT # from the MTO; the rest is printed: END OF MISSION, TGT# …, 1 TANK DESTROYED/NEUTRALIZED, ESTIMATED 3 CASUALTIES.
